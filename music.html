Hey, I'm Thomas. This is my album! This entire webpage! This website is a permanent record of my music being created and shared. I want to run it a bit like a custom social media platform where I can just post music and anyone can listen instantly for free. Thank you to Boycott Daylight (Zane Warsen, Jack Klein, Owen Peters, James Ernst), Liam Shutt, Cameron King, Griffin Breshears, David Fedor, Liam McDougal, Andrew Bernard, Nadia Gremillion, Garrett Beecher, Andrew Hamson, my dad Paul Choboter, Ryan Morris, Cooper Durkey, Luca Carnevale, Alex Ong, Ben Geil, Marley Paltiel, Chris Marston, Max Chalekson, David Frismodt, Josh Baum, Owen Daulton, Elijah Esparza, David Landers, Pat Monson, and everyone else who has supported me, encouraged me to keep going, or jammed with me. You profoundly shaped my musical journey. This music would not exist in its current form without you. The first song is "After the End." The last one is "Infrastructure," which is also the most recently recorded; songs auto-play upwards instead of downwards, because this is my website and I can do things however I want to. Enjoy the journey! Spread joy!
