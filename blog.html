I Just Made My First Blog Post!

September 23, 2025

Hello, world! This is so exciting, I have my very own blog now! There are a few things I have been wanting to talk about. I love learning and I love teaching. I love engaging in passionate conversations about important topics. Discussions will ensue on this webpage! I plan on implementing a comments section eventually that will let people post questions, critiques, compliments, or insults anonymously. This should be interesting!

I want to mainly focus on computing and electronic technology, as well as music, philosophy, and math. We will cover a lot of stuff on this blog. Stay tuned! I will post on my socials when there is something new on here. Expect updates once a week or so. (Unlikely but I can dream)
